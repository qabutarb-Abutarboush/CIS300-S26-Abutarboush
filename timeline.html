1059

Papal Election Reform

In 1059, Pope Nicholas II changed the way popes were chosen by giving the College of Cardinals a stronger role in papal elections. This weakened the influence of emperors and Roman nobles over the papacy. It helped set the stage for the Investiture Conflict because the Church was becoming more independent from secular rulers.
